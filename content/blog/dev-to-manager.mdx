## The wrong lessons

I thought leading meant having the answers. It doesn't. It means making sure the team has the context, the tools, and the safety to find answers faster than you could alone.

## The numbers

- 12 engineers across Wendy's, Takeda, Stellantis, Sifera
- Team productivity up 25% through mentoring
- Sprint delivery 86% → 94% after taking over at FortressOS

## The real lesson

Mentoring compounds. Every hour spent teaching someone to fish paid back in weeks I didn't spend debugging their fishing nets.
